# StoryBrand Messaging

Donald Miller's 7-part framework. The customer is the hero. You are the guide. Never make yourself the hero.

## The 7 elements

### 1. A character (the hero = the customer)
What do they want? One thing, stated concretely. Not "grow your business" — "sign 3 new clients this month."

### 2. Has a problem
Three layers, all named:
- **External**: the visible obstacle ("my website doesn't convert")
- **Internal**: how it makes them feel ("I feel like a fraud when the site looks amateur")
- **Philosophical**: why it's wrong ("small businesses deserve premium design too")

Address all three in your copy. Most brands only address external.

### 3. Meets a guide (you)
Show two things:
- **Empathy**: "We know how it feels when..."
- **Authority**: "We've done this 47 times for founders like you"

Never lead with your history, awards, or years in business. Lead with understanding.

### 4. Who gives them a plan
3-4 concrete steps. Numbered. Reduces anxiety. "Book a call → We audit your site → You get a redesign in 21 days."

### 5. And calls them to action
Two CTAs on every page:
- **Direct**: "Book a call" / "Buy now"
- **Transitional** (for the not-ready): "Download the free guide"

Direct CTA in header, hero, and footer. Same words, always.

### 6. That helps them avoid failure
Name what's at stake if they don't act. "Every month without X = $Y lost." Loss aversion is 2x stronger than gain seeking.

### 7. And ends in success
Paint the specific end state. Not "you'll be successful" — "your calendar full of qualified calls by month 3."

## Landing page structure (StoryBrand order)

1. **Hero section**: what you offer + the one thing they want (one sentence each) + direct CTA
2. **The stakes**: what's wrong now / what happens if nothing changes
3. **Value stack**: 3-4 benefits (customer-outcomes, not your features)
4. **The guide**: brief credibility, empathy first
5. **The plan**: 3-step process
6. **Transitional CTA**: for the not-ready
7. **Explanatory paragraph**: for the researcher
8. **Video** (optional): 90 seconds, same story
9. **Junk drawer** (footer): everything you cut

## The one-liner test
> "We help [customer] who wants [thing] by [method], so they can [outcome], and avoid [stake]."

If you can't fill this in for your business in 30 seconds, your messaging is broken. Fix that first.

## Anti-patterns
- Header full of "we/us/our" instead of "you/your"
- Feature list before benefit summary
- Founder bio above the fold
- No stated CTA (or 5 competing ones)
- Copy about your process instead of their outcome
