# Made to Stick

Chip & Dan Heath's SUCCESs framework. Sticky ideas share 6 traits. Aim for at least 3 per message.

## S — Simple
Find the core. What's the one thing that must survive?
- Southwest Airlines: "THE low-fare airline." Every decision made downstream.
- Bill Clinton: "It's the economy, stupid."
- Your product: `[what's your 3-word core?]`

Not simplification — prioritization. Cut everything until removing one more thing breaks the message.

## U — Unexpected
Break a pattern to grab attention. Sustain interest with a curiosity gap.
- "Which airline has the best safety record?" → open a loop
- Statistics that violate assumption ("Only 2% of X, not 50%")
- Counterintuitive framing ("Selling less makes you more money — here's how")

Rule: identify the conventional wisdom, then break it in the opening line.

## C — Concrete
Sensory, specific, testable. "A cool refreshing drink" is abstract. "Ice-cold Coke on a July afternoon" is concrete.
- Replace concepts with objects ("increase engagement" → "reply within 3 minutes")
- Give numbers ("increase" → "3x")
- Name people ("customers say" → "Maria, a nurse in Denver, told us")

Test: can two strangers picture the same thing after reading it?

## C — Credible
Not authority alone — internal credibility. Ways to earn belief:
- Specific detail ("6,847 users" > "thousands of users")
- Named source ("The Wall Street Journal, March 12")
- Testable claim ("Try it in 30 seconds")
- Antiauthority ("We used to do this wrong — here's what changed")
- Vivid detail (a war story with texture)

## E — Emotional
Make people feel, not just think. Two moves:
- **Association**: link to something already emotional (family, pride, freedom)
- **Self-interest**: name what's in it for them, concretely

Statistics rarely move. Stories about one person always do. Mother Teresa: "If I look at the mass, I will never act. If I look at the one, I will."

## S — Stories
Story = simulation + inspiration. Three plot templates that always work:
- **Challenge**: David vs Goliath, underdog vs obstacle
- **Connection**: strangers becoming allies, bridging divides
- **Creativity**: new solution to old problem (MacGyver plot)

Frame every founding story, case study, and pitch as one of these three.

## Sticky test
For any tagline, hook, or big claim:
- Simple? (one thing)
- Unexpected? (breaks a pattern)
- Concrete? (can I picture it?)
- Credible? (would I believe it from a stranger?)
- Emotional? (do I feel something?)
- Story? (does it hint at a narrative?)

3+ yeses = ship it. Fewer = rewrite.

## Villain: the Curse of Knowledge
You know so much you can't imagine not knowing. It's why experts write badly. Cure: write for a specific person who knows nothing about it, and name them.
