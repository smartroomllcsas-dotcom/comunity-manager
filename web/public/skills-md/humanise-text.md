# Humanise Text

AI text has fingerprints. Remove them.

## The 20 dead giveaways

1. `delve` — never say this
2. `leverage` (as a verb) — use `use`
3. `seamless(ly)` — cut the word
4. `robust` — cut
5. `holistic` — cut
6. `comprehensive` — cut
7. `crucial` / `essential` / `paramount` — usually cut
8. `it's important to note that` — cut, or say the thing
9. `in the ever-evolving landscape of` — burn it
10. `unlock the power of` — burn it
11. `at its core` — cut
12. `game-changer` / `revolutionize` — cut
13. `moreover` / `furthermore` / `additionally` — start a new sentence instead
14. Opening summary sentence ("In this article we'll explore...") — cut
15. Closing summary paragraph ("In conclusion...") — cut
16. Numbered lists where every item is exactly the same length
17. Tricolons everywhere: "clear, concise, and compelling" — kill 2 of 3
18. Stacked em-dashes — one per paragraph max
19. Every paragraph 3 sentences, similar length
20. Hedging: "can", "may", "often", "generally" — pick a stance

## Structural tells
- Perfect parallelism in every list
- Section headings that are all noun phrases the same length
- Introductions that restate the prompt
- Bulleted lists in the middle of prose

## The humanise pass (do in this order)

1. **Cut all opening/closing summaries** — start on the point, end on the point
2. **Break parallel structure** in lists — one item short, one long
3. **Add specificity**: swap "many users" → "one client, Maria, told us"
4. **Insert one strong opinion** per 200 words
5. **Break at least one 'rule'**: sentence fragment. Or a 40-word runner-sentence.
6. **Read aloud** — anywhere you'd re-breathe unnaturally, rewrite
7. **Cut 20%** — always cuts, never adds

## What NOT to strip
- Precision (specific numbers, dates, names)
- Structure that genuinely aids comprehension
- Technical accuracy

## The tell that survives everything
Text that has no first-person opinion, no example from lived experience, no rough edge = AI even if every word is fixed. Add one paragraph that only a real person could have written.
